❓ How to Play Hearthwall

Goal: Build a thriving town, advance through the four ages, raise an army and destroy every enemy building and unit. That is the only way to win. (If you choose Victory: Classic in the main menu, a Wonder kept standing for 300 seconds or every Relic held for 300 seconds also wins, for you and for the computer; a red banner then counts down.)

🛒 Trade

Build a second Market far from your first (or team up with an ally who has one), train Trade Carts at a Market, select them and right-click the far Market. They walk back and forth and bring gold home every trip; the farther apart the Markets, the more gold. Trade Cogs do the same between Docks. Caravan (at the Market) makes them faster.

🐑 Sheep

Sheep belong to whoever finds them: a sheep becomes yours when one of your units comes close. Select your sheep and right-click to walk them next to your Town Center, then have villagers eat them there (short walks = fast food). Watch out: an enemy unit that finds your sheep with none of your units nearby steals them.

🤝 Teams and 🌐 online play

Teams: in the main menu, put each player on a team (or leave everyone on No team for all against all). Allies share their vision, never hurt each other (except with mangonel blasts, as in AoE2), start next to each other, and win or lose together: your team loses only when every ally is out.

Online (up to 4 people): works on the web link of Hearthwall (not from the Hearthwall.bat / file copy). One person presses 🌐 Host online game and gets a 5-letter code (or a link to copy); the others open the same web link, type the code under Join with code (or open the host's link) and are in. The computers connect directly to each other; some school, work or hotel networks block that (a phone hotspot usually works). On the published claude.ai page, share the page by name from its Share menu instead; there the others press Join. The host picks the map, the number of computer players, the teams and the settings, then presses Start. Windows, Mac and any browser can play together. Everyone's orders reach the others a moment later. P pauses the game for everybody. If a friend's page closes, the computer plays for them until they open the page again and press Rejoin. Keep the host's tab open: when the host leaves, the game ends. Saving to a file is not available in online games.

🌾 Economy

Villagers gather 🍖 food (berries, farms), 🪵 wood (trees), 🪙 gold and 🪨 stone (mines). They carry it to the nearest drop-off: the Town Center, a Lumber Camp (wood), a Mining Camp (gold and stone) or a Mill (food). Build drop-offs close to resources!
Every unit takes 1 population. Houses add 5 room each. The single most important habit: always keep your Town Center training villagers.

🎖️ Army orders

Select soldiers to see more buttons: Attack Move (walk somewhere and fight everything on the way), Patrol, a Stance button (each click steps through Aggressive, Defensive, Stand Ground, No Attack) and a Formation button (Line puts melee in front and archers behind, Box, Spread; groups in formation move at the speed of their slowest unit). The Delete key removes the selected units. Gates: place one on your own wall; your units walk through, enemies cannot.

🔔 Defending

Your Town Center only shoots arrows when units are inside. When raiders come, click it and press Ring Town Bell: villagers run into the nearest Town Center, Tower or Castle, and every villager inside adds an arrow. Press All Clear to send them back to work. Soldiers can also garrison (Garrison button, or Alt + right-click a building) and heal slowly inside.

⛪ Monks, Relics and the Market

In the Castle Age a Monastery trains Monks. They heal nearby units by themselves; right-click an enemy unit to convert it to your side (it takes a few seconds, then the monk must rest for a minute). Monks also pick up the 5 golden Relics on the map: each Relic in your Monastery makes gold forever, and holding all of them for 300 seconds wins the game. The Market (Feudal Age) buys and sells food, wood and stone for gold. The University researches stronger buildings and towers.

⛵ Water

Build a Dock on the shore. Fishing Ships catch fish and build Fish Traps (floating farms). Warships: Galleys shoot arrows, Fire Galleys burn ships up close, Demolition Rafts explode on ships and shore buildings.
On the Islands map you need a Transport Ship to reach the enemy: click your transport and press Load soldiers (every idle soldier within 10 tiles gets on board, 10 per ship), or select soldiers and right-click the ship (what does not fit goes to your other transports nearby). Then select the transports and right-click the enemy shore: they sail there, unload, and the soldiers walk to the spot you clicked.

⬆️ The Four Ages

Dark Age → Feudal Age (500🍖) → Castle Age (800🍖 200🪙) → Imperial Age (1000🍖 800🪙). Click the Town Center to advance. Each age unlocks stronger units, buildings and upgrades.

🏳️ Civilizations

🪓 Valdmark: infantry +1 attack, Berserkers · 🏹 Ardenne: archers +1 range, Longbowmen · 🏇 Khazari: cavalry +20% HP, Horse Archers · 🐴 Tessaly: faster gathering, Cataphracts · 🛡️ Hohenmark: +1 armor for infantry and cavalry, Ironclad Knights · 🐪 Zarqan: cheap market, Mamelukes · 🏹 Kaiyuan: cheaper technologies, Chu Ko Nu · 🏇 Iberra: fast builders, Conquistadors. Each civilization also has two unique technologies at its Castle.

🗺️ Maps

Continental: lakes and woods. Arabia: open land with small woods and no water: fights start early. Black Forest: forest everywhere except your clearing and a few winding paths: easy to wall, slow to attack. Islands: one island each, you need ships. Play against up to 3 computer opponents (everyone against everyone).
